The Market Problem & Our Solution

The Global Nutrition Blindspot: Mainstream nutrition apps like MyFitnessPal and YAZIO were built for Western diets. When 1.4+ billion people in Africa and the 150M+ global diaspora try to track authentic dishes (Amala, Jollof, Moi Moi, Egusi, Fufu, Waakye), existing apps fail completely.

The MealOptimizer Innovation: We combine computer vision AI calibrated for traditional African preparations with a clinical medical engine (Type-2 Diabetes, Hypertension, CKD, PCOS), turning cultural meals into powerful metabolic medicine.

🍲 Culturally-Accurate Vision AI

Instantly identifies traditional ingredients, resistant starches, palm oil balances, and fiber content that Western apps miss.

🩺 Clinical Condition Engine

Real-time glycemic spike warnings for diabetics, sodium checks for hypertension, and potassium alerts for kidney health.

💬 WhatsApp AI Food Bot

Zero app friction: users text or snap a meal on WhatsApp, and Gemini AI auto-logs calories and macros to their account.

🏥 1-Tap Doctor Visit PDF Report

Transforms 30-day vitals, projected eA1c, and food logs into a clinical summary formatted for hospital and dietitian visits.
