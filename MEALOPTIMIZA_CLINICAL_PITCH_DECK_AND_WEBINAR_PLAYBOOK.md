## 1. Executive Summary & Presentation Strategy

Healthcare practitioners managing diabetic, hypertensive, and metabolic syndrome patients face a critical challenge: **over 82% of patients fail traditional paper food journals within 7 days**, and standard clinical dietary guidelines often alienate African and Caribbean diaspora populations by labeling cultural staples as "unhealthy."

**MealOptimiza bridges this clinical gap.** By pairing cutting-edge AI food scanning with culturally validated biochemical protocols (viscous mucilage gel barriers, glycemic food sequencing, KDIGO renal leaching), MealOptimiza empowers patients to enjoy their heritage foods safely while providing clinicians with automated, objective 14-day metabolic dossiers.

For the medical practice, MealOptimiza turns passive patient consultations into active **Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)** touchpoints and recurring digital care memberships, generating **\$42,000 to \$126,000+ in annual recurring practice revenue per 100–300 enrolled patients**.

1. **Connect Smartphone via Zoom Screen Share:** Use AirPlay (iOS) or USB Cable (Android/Mac/PC) to share the smartphone screen directly.
2. **Engage the Room:** Ask in chat: *"Who has a patient struggling with high blood sugar on a traditional diet? Type their favorite dinner into the chat."*
3. **Execute Live Scan:** Select an answer (e.g. *Eba + Egusi Soup + Fried Plantain*). Snap photo or input via WhatsApp bot.
4. **Highlight the 3 Core Output Signals:**
